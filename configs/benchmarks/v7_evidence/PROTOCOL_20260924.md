## E5 — Is tail calibration part of the Gaussian-noise failure?
Arm `NA_mad` (MAD instead of tail q95), MVTec AD full15, clean + stored Gaussian
s3 root. relief = drop(NA_mad) − drop(NA_gated), control drop −20.687.
- relief ≥ 3.0pp → tail calibration carries part of the failure.
- relief < 3.0pp → it does not; with A1 (shallow, +1.646) this makes "no
  support-side component carries the noise failure" hold for both components.

## E7 — Does the 1-shot estimator switch explain the 1-shot drop?
MVTec AD 2 `test_public`, 2-shot nested manifest (seed 0), arms gated and
deep-only with `loo_estimator: token` versus the existing `source` runs.
Δ_est = AUPRO(token) − AUPRO(source), `regular` stratum, gated arm.
Observed 1-shot drop for gated (2→1 shot, regular) = 4.88pp.
- Δ_est ≤ −2.44pp → the estimator switch is implicated; open a 1-shot estimator line.
- Δ_est > −2.44pp → close the line; the 1-shot drop is attributed to support scarcity.

### E10 — Other methods under MVTec AD 2 acquisition shift
No stored artifact compares any external method across the MVTec AD 2
`test_public` acquisition strata (regular, underexposed, overexposed, shift_1–3);
only our own ablations have per-stratum metrics, and the official-server rows use
a different pipeline and reported comparator numbers. Table 1's 16-shot rows also
used different support sets per method (RSG3: `SD_support_16`; SuperAD-16 and
RN-FMLK-16: a SuperAD-selected manifest; others: seed-0 random draws).
- Design: MVTec AD 2 `test_public`, 16-shot, ONE shared manifest for every method
  (`configs/methods/VOID_support_16.json`, file-identical to `SD_support_16`),
  frozen backbones as each method's baseline config specifies, a method-agnostic
  per-stratum evaluator (same streaming metric contract, masks evaluator-only).
  Methods: RegAD, RD4AD, PatchCore, FastRecon, RN-FMLK, SuperAD (DINOv2), SuperAD
  (DINOv3), and RSG3 as control.
- C0: RSG3's per-stratum AUPRO reproduces the stored void-instrumentation 16-shot
  gated cells (e.g. regular 61.26, shift_1 59.40) within 0.05pp.
- Claim rule: "RSG3 leads under acquisition shift" iff RSG3 − best other method
  > 0.86pp on `shift_1` AND on at least 2 of the 3 shift strata; otherwise the
  actual ranking is reported. Degradation (stratum − regular) is reported for every
  method separately; "smallest drop" is claimed only with a > 0.86pp margin.

### E5 erratum (post hoc, 2026-09-25 — recorded after the E5 result was inspected)
The E5 formula `relief = drop(NA_mad) − drop(NA_gated)` has its sign inverted relative to its stated meaning
("relief" = how much replacing tail calibration reduces the drop, i.e. `drop(NA_gated) − drop(NA_mad)`).
The rule text above is left unchanged. The verdict is taken by the stated meaning: observed
drop(NA_mad) 23.872 vs drop(NA_gated) 20.687 → relief −3.185 < 3.0 → tail calibration does not carry the failure.
This is a post-hoc correction of a transcription error, not a pre-registered decision.

### E5 correction to the post-hoc erratum (2026-09-25)
The preceding erratum used positive drop magnitudes and incorrectly called the registered
formula inverted. It is superseded here; the original signed convention and rule remain
the pre-registered test. With `drop = noisy AUPRO − clean AUPRO`, the control is
`−20.687` as recorded above and `drop(NA_mad) = −23.872`. Thus the registered
`relief = drop(NA_mad) − drop(NA_gated) = −3.185pp < 3.0pp`: replacing q95 with
MAD did not meet the recovery criterion and worsened the observed drop. This
single replacement, together with A1, does not establish that no support-side
component causes the noise failure.

### E7 outcome interpretation (post hoc, 2026-09-25)
The registered gate uses the `regular` stratum. A prior E7 report used whole-split
AUPRO. Its corrected regular-stratum gated Δ_est is `+0.53307513pp > −2.44pp`,
so the estimator-switch gate remains unmet. The gate does not identify support
scarcity as the exclusive cause of the 1-shot drop; that attribution in the
registered rule is a hypothesis, not an experimentally isolated conclusion.
