# SO-CFM Stage -1 Evidence Boundary

## Why this branch exists

Earlier support-only interventions showed that controlled photometric
directions are partly identifiable, but static projection, invariant-channel
selection, conditional normalization, and bank expansion either removed too
little shifted-normal tail or absorbed defect evidence. The new variable is a
time-dependent normal transport operator with an explicit anomaly-persistence
guard and causal hard nulls.

## Nearest negative precedents

- CF-NTR: physical tangent specificity passed, but held-shift drift reduction
  missed its gate and synthetic defect retention was only about 0.93.
- DINO invariant-channel readout: held-shift displacement fell, but local and
  broad defect retention collapsed.
- GB-CMF: a prior feature-matching objective destroyed geometry and passed no
  continuation gate.

SO-CFM is therefore required to learn only normal velocity targets, preserve a
raw-feature path/geometry, use synthetic anomalies solely as a guard in the
candidate arm, and beat both a discrete orbit bank and shuffled-mechanism
controls. Synthetic success alone cannot justify a real anomaly-detection
claim.
